# Operator guide — CUB

Portal: no self-service e-procurement/FDI portal -- proposals are
identified/presented to MINCEX (Ministerio del Comercio Exterior y la
Inversión Extranjera), admitted (Decreto 325/2014 Reglamento Art. 33),
referred to the Comisión de Evaluación de Negocios con Inversión
Extranjera (Art. 34), then routed by MINCEX to whichever of Ley 118
Art. 21's three approval authorities (Consejo de Estado / Consejo de
Ministros / a delegated jefe de organismo) has jurisdiction over the
proposal's declared sector/modality. Entity: Registro Mercantil
(Ministerio de Justicia, Decreto-Ley 226/2001) + ONAT NIT (Número de
Identificación Tributaria) tax record. Filings that claim the wrong
approval-authority tier for their own declared sector/modality also
clear the Ley 118 Art. 21 authority-jurisdiction gate before a filing
can proceed. Human-gated filing only.
